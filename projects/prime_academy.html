01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

A foundation
for every learner.

Prime Academy is a Chinese and English education and child development centre focused on nurturing confident, curious and lifelong learners. Its guidelines connect positioning and values with a clear identity system across logo, color, typography, photography and space.

POSITIONING / VISION

A Chinese & English learning centre nurturing confident, curious and globally minded children through systematic curriculum and professional teaching.

The guidelines describe an ambition to become Cambodia's most trusted Chinese & English Education and Child Development Centre, supported by curriculum, teacher development, personalized learning and family partnership.
